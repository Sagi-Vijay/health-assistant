## Option 1: Technical Showcase (Recommended)

🩺 Excited to share my latest project: **AI Health Assistant** - A full-stack healthcare companion powered by Google's Gemini 1.5 Pro!

🚀 **Key Features:**
✅ Symptom analysis with RAG-powered diagnosis suggestions
✅ Medical report analysis (PDF parsing + AI summarization)
✅ Voice-enabled health queries (multimodal AI)
✅ Secure user authentication & medical history tracking
✅ Doctor dashboard for patient summaries

🛠️ **Tech Stack:**
• Backend: FastAPI + LangChain
• LLM: Google Gemini 1.5 Pro
• Vector DB: FAISS (RAG pipeline)
• Frontend: Streamlit
• Auth: JWT with OAuth2

This project demonstrates the power of LLMs in healthcare while maintaining strict safety guardrails to ensure responsible AI usage.

🔗 Check out the code: https://github.com/Sagi-Vijay/health-assistant
